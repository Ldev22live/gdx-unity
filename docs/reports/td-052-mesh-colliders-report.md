**Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Decision:** TD-052 (approved: "TD-052 approved", as typed) · **Start and end HEAD:** `f9849c2` (nothing committed; the working tree already held many uncommitted changes of the owner and earlier phases, left as they were)
**Asked:** "TD-052 approved". The open questions were not answered, so the proposals were built (all mesh nodes merged, rest pose, warning above 100,000 triangles).

## What was built

- **Components.** `ConvexHullCollider3DComponent` and `MeshCollider3DComponent` + serializers (`engine-scene`); `MeshGeometry3D`; `ColliderGeometrySource` (read by `ColliderGeometry` in `engine-render`: glTF model in rest pose, or mesh primitive), registered with the scene by `SceneRenderer3D`.
- **Backend.** `ShapeDescriptor3D` kinds `HULL` and `MESH`; Jolt `ConvexHullShapeSettings` and `MeshShapeSettings` in `JoltPhysicsBackend3D`; hulls above 64 points are reduced.
- **Rules.** A mesh collider on a dynamic or kinematic body, an entity with no Model or Mesh Primitive, a model that failed to load, a mesh without triangles and a scene that cannot read meshes are refused with the entity named.
- **Editor.** Add Component > Physics 3D > Convex Hull Collider 3D / Mesh Collider 3D (refused without a mesh, and a mesh collider on a moving body), Details editors, lines in the 3D view (hull: crosses at its points; mesh: triangle edges).
- **Game runtime.** Serializers registered in `GameComponentSerializers` (28 types).
- **Sample.** `physics-mesh-3d` (generated by `tools/make_mesh_collider_sample.py`): a glTF terrain with a ramp, a plateau and a bowl as a Mesh Collider 3D; balls roll off the plateau, boxes drop into the bowl, rocks with a hull built from their mesh land on the ramp.

## Problems found on the way

- The sphere primitive's triangles first faced inwards (caught by the winding test); fixed.
- A hull cube settles about 2 units into the floor (Jolt's penetration slop); the test tolerance says so.
- My first sample had balls on the flat plateau, where they simply stay; they now start on the slope.
- The sample's rock rotation was not a unit quaternion; the generator now normalizes it.

## Known limitations and next steps

- Triangle meshes on static bodies only; all mesh nodes of a model merged into one shape; rest pose only; scale read at physics start.
- A hull of more than 64 points is reduced and can be slightly smaller than the mesh.
- The 100,000-triangle warning is collected (`PhysicsSystem3D.getWarnings()`) but not shown in the Output Log.
- No character in the sample (covered by a real-Jolt test); the trigger flag on a mesh collider, skinned models and textured models are untested.
- The web (Babylon) player ignores the components, with no warning yet.
- Web tests (`npm ci`, `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`) still not run (disk space).
