# OpenGameArt sample game upgrades

- **Platformer Profile 3D** now draws Detective Bunny under Jumper and has a Hex Paver B starting surface. The original collider, camera, input, floating stone route and flag remain in the scene. Movement and jumping continue to come from the existing profile.
- **Action RPG Valley** replaces the blocking cube boulder with imported rock geometry on the same collider, adds two decorative outcrops and displays animated fox and rabbit wildlife. The original terrain, third-person movement and camera remain in use. The selected lion source needs production work and the RPG player remains a box.
- **Action RPG Isometric** uses the Hex Paver B material on the low step. Its collider and courtyard gameplay are preserved.

The imported models are normal scene assets and appear in the local Content Browser. Search by `provider:opengameart`, then narrow by `species:rabbit` or `role:wildlife`. The paver material can be assigned in Details. The 2D samples, puzzle samples and other genres retain their prior assets.
