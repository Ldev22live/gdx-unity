# OpenGameArt source audit

OpenGameArt discovery used indexed listing excerpts. ForgeRoom TD-094 requires a person to retrieve the files. Licensing below is listing evidence. File attribution, contents, and technical status remain pending until the archives are inspected.

| Candidate | Listing author and license | Intended use and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| [Mansedj](https://opengameart.org/content/mansedj-blender-279-blender-361) | Djsedj, CC0 | Action RPG Valley protagonist candidate. Anthropomorphic lion with a rig and head, body and eye textures. The older bundle reports over 210,000 body and head faces before modifiers. Inspect the newer Blender 3.6.1 archive first. Verify topology, skin weights, clothes, and runtime clips. |
| [PBR Materials (Rocks)](https://opengameart.org/content/pbr-materials-rocks) | KillGorack, CC0 | Evaluate rockwall.zip for RPG valley and platformer stone. The description cites a separate grass source for one paver variant, so that variant needs its own rights review. |
| [Realistic Rocks/Boulders](https://opengameart.org/content/realistic-rocksboulders) | WeaponGuy, CC0 | Blender source for background rocks. Check linked images and modern material conversion before considering it for import. |
| [3TD Conifer Forest Pack v1.1](https://opengameart.org/content/3td-conifer-forest-pack-v11) | Ron Kapaun, submitted by hreikin, CC0 | Forest environment candidate. DAE and associated textures require conversion and dependency validation. |
| [Detective Bunny](https://opengameart.org/content/detective-bunny) | atornity, CC0 | Platformer candidate. Rigged bipedal rabbit with Walk, Talk, Idle, Climb and Investigate. Inspect style, polygon count and export. Run, jump, fall and land need authoring or retargeting. |
| [Rabbit](https://opengameart.org/content/rabbit-0) | CDmir and TinyWorlds, CC0 | Rigged forest animal with diffuse and normal maps. Evaluate as wildlife after checking the rig, scale and visuals. |
| [Fox](https://opengameart.org/content/fox-0) | br-n518, CC0 | 342-triangle animated animal with 1K maps. Its morphology and visual fidelity fall short of the protagonist target. |
| [Fox Bases](https://opengameart.org/content/fox-bases-player-character-sheet) | Listing offers CC BY 4.0 among its choices | Anthropomorphic 2D base. Needs license approval under TD-094, production artwork and additional animation states. |
| [Door Open SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/door-open-sfx) | Oiboo, CC0 | Possible RPG interaction cue. WAV fits the existing recorded-audio path. |

The [CC0 3D collection](https://opengameart.org/content/3d-assets-cc0) and [Realistic 3D RPG CC0 collection](https://opengameart.org/content/realistic-3d-rpg-cc0) are discovery indexes. The individual file listing and bundled license determine eligibility. See the [CC0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode).

The [OpenGameArt inventory](opengameart-inventory.md) records the subsequent local inspection and import outcomes.
